# Academic search log: post-1998 tests of Dow Theory

Searched 2026-11-08 via WebSearch. Conclusion: no peer-reviewed or working-paper test of the Industrials and Transports confirmation rule or Dow Theory signals was found after Brown, Goetzmann and Kumar (1998).

## Queries
1. Dow Theory empirical test, Dow Jones Industrial and Transportation average confirmation trading rule, out-of-sample journal.
2. "Dow Theory" SSRN working paper profitability test, confirmation signals.
3. "Dow theory" trading strategy, Jensen alpha, Sharpe ratio, Transportation Average predictive power.
4. Dow Theory test in emerging markets (India, China, Japan), confirmation, empirical evidence.
5. "Dow Theory" "trend confirmation" or "Dow Theory signals", two indices, cointegration or momentum, arXiv, SSRN, Journal of Technical Analysis.
6. A Chinese-language query on empirical tests of Dow Theory confirmation.
7. Brown, Goetzmann and Kumar replication or follow-up, 1930-1997 or 2000s.
8. Sideris and Russell thesis queries, and Cowles 1944 and Dimand and Veloce queries.
