## Notes
Only the first 200k of 423k characters were read; "NOT MENTIONED" means not found in that portion. Bishop (1960) states Dow did not use the word "line" for the averages and had no confirmation principle in his editorials, consistent with absence here. Nelson edited and compiled; the three-eighths phrasing at Ch. IX may be Nelson's gloss. Nelson coined "Dow's Theory" as footnote label; Dow never did.
